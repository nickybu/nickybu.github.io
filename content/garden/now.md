I moved to London in September 2025 to start a PhD at the Francis Crick Institute and King's College London with the [Sadeh Lab](https://sadehlab.com/) and  [Marin Lab](devneuro.org.uk/marinlab/), exploring biologically-inspired learning algorithms that enable networks to adapt and generalise to learn many different things.
- put more technically, I'm looking at the role of cortical interneurons (mainly in mice and humans) in multitask/continual learning, particularly interested in somatostatin+ (SST) interneurons and dendritic computations.
